Show Bar Support

Help with the Mac App Store edition of Show Bar, including screen capture and clipboard history.

Contact support

Email na0ryank0@gmail.com with your question. To help us troubleshoot, include your macOS version, the steps you took, and any error message you saw. Please do not include private clipboard contents or screenshots unless they are needed to explain the issue.

Common questions

How do I capture a screenshot?

Open Show Bar and choose Capture Screen. If macOS asks, allow Screen Recording access in System Settings, then return to Show Bar and try again.

How do I save clipboard text?

Copy text as usual, then choose Add Current Text in Show Bar. Text is added only when you press that button and stays in the app's local history. Choose Clear History to remove saved text.

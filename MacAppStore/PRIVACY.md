# Show Bar for Mac App Store — Privacy Policy

Show Bar stores clipboard text that you explicitly add to its history on your Mac. You can copy an item back to the clipboard or clear the history in the app. The history is kept in the app's local macOS sandbox and is not sent to Naor Yanko or any other server.

Show Bar captures a display only after you choose **Capture Screen**. macOS may ask you to grant Screen Recording access. Captured screenshots remain in memory until you close the app unless you choose **Save PNG**. Saving uses the macOS save panel and writes only to the location you select. Copying a screenshot places it on the system clipboard.

Show Bar does not include analytics, advertising, accounts, or third-party tracking.

For privacy questions, contact [na0ryank0@gmail.com](mailto:na0ryank0@gmail.com).

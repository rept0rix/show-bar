Show Bar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy describes the Mac App Store edition of Show Bar.

Clipboard history

Show Bar reads plain text from the system clipboard only when you press “Add Current Text.” Text you choose to add is stored locally in the app's macOS sandbox. You can copy an item back to the clipboard or clear the history in the app. Show Bar does not send clipboard contents to a server.

Screen capture

Show Bar captures the main display only after you press “Capture Screen.” macOS may ask you to grant Screen Recording access. A captured image remains in memory until you close the app unless you choose to save it. Saving uses the macOS save panel and writes only to the location you select. Copying a screenshot places it on the system clipboard.

Data collection

Show Bar does not include accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking. It does not transmit app data to Naor Yanko or other servers.

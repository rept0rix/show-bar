Five Superpowers In One App

Everything your Mac was missing.

Instead of running 5 separate background daemons and paying monthly subscriptions, Show Bar combines the most wanted window management and productivity tools in one native binary.

DOCK PREVIEWS

Hover the Dock to peek inside.

Windows has had taskbar previews since Windows 7. Show Bar finally brings this to macOS: hover any app icon on your Dock to view live thumbnails of all its open windows.

  • Instant hover trigger with zero WindowServer lag
  • Switch directly to any window across multiple Spaces or external monitors
  • Supports Bottom, Left, and Right Dock placements
WINDOW-LEVEL SWITCHER

Command-Tab to windows, not just apps.

Tired of cycling through five Safari windows blind? Show Bar replaces the native Command-Tab switcher with crisp live previews of every active window so you immediately land where you want.

  • Instant tap switches between your last two windows immediately
  • Filter and select via Arrow keys, Numbers, or Mouse hover
  • Shows Desktop / Space tags for windows on other virtual screens
WINDOW SNAPPING

Snap and tile in a single keystroke.

Organize your screen space without clunky mouse gymnastics. Press Control-Option with arrow keys to tile windows into left/right halves, corners, thirds, or maximize.

  • Quick split: Left half, right half, top, bottom, or fullscreen
  • Lightweight and native with zero battery drain
  • Move windows effortlessly across multiple displays
CLIPBOARD HISTORY

Never lose what you copied.

Press Control-Option-V to open your visual clipboard shelf. Search through recent copies, code snippets, formatted text, links, and photos, and paste them back in a snap.

  • Fast fuzzy search to retrieve past clips in seconds
  • 100% on-device local storage — nothing ever touches the cloud
  • Plain text, formatted rich text, links, and image support
SCREENSHOT SHELF & MARKUP

Pin, markup, and batch paste screenshots.

Whenever you capture a screenshot, Show Bar pins it temporarily in the corner. Annotate with arrows and callouts, crop with precision, or collect multiple captures and paste them all together in one go.

  • Floating corner shelf keeps recent captures at your fingertips
  • Full markup editor: arrows, text, shapes, blur, and cropping
  • Copy All: sends multiple captured images in a single paste

Quick Start

How to install in 30 seconds.

Show Bar is notarized by Apple so you never encounter Gatekeeper warnings or quarantine errors.

1

Download & Open

Click the Download button to grab Show-Bar-1.9.4.dmg. Double-click the downloaded file in your Downloads folder to mount the disk image.

2

Drag to Applications

Drag the Show Bar app icon into the Applications folder shortcut shown inside the installer window.

3

Launch & Enjoy

Open Show Bar from your Applications folder or press Cmd + Space and type Show Bar. Follow the one-time macOS permission prompts for Screen Recording and Accessibility to enable live previews.
